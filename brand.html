00 · Origin, Mission & Ethos

Where TRACE came from

The brand begins with a question the body had already answered. Everything that follows, the mark, the palette, the voice, exists to make that answer feel like care.

2026 · Origin

A question no one could answer

TRACE began with a simple frustration. Our founder, a digital marketer and a designer, kept meeting people who could describe their day in detail but could not explain how they actually felt. The body had been keeping a record all along. Nobody was reading it.

The first prototype was a sensor taped to a wrist and a week of data nobody knew how to interpret. What surprised us was not the numbers. It was the moment a colleague looked at her own stress curve and said, quietly, "so it already knew."

We spent months building something worthy of that sentence.

Mission

To give every person a clear picture of themselves, and give it to no one else.

Understanding is the product. Confidentiality is the promise that makes it possible. TRACE exists so that no one has to guess how they are doing.

01

The body speaks first You feel it before you can name it. TRACE listens at that level, so nothing important goes unsaid.

02

Understanding, not judgement We measure to reveal, never to score. There is no failing week.

03

Yours alone Everything TRACE knows belongs to one person. Confidentiality is not a setting, it is the architecture.

04

Quiet by default TRACE speaks only when it matters. Care that constantly interrupts is not care.

The pulse mark and wordmark

The mark is five rounded pulse bars, a heartbeat rendered as data with a single Olympian blue centre bar. The wordmark is Inter Light, tracked wide, always uppercase. The ™ sits at the cap-height corner in platinum.

05 · Voice

Calm, certain, close

You, understood.

TRACE speaks the way it senses: quietly, and with total certainty. Sentences are short. There is no hype, no jargon, and never an exclamation mark. It speaks to one person rather than an audience, and it never congratulates, scolds, or asks what it already knows. The full stop does the work that volume does elsewhere. Calm, final, certain.

PreciseWarmUnhurriedConfidentPersonal

We say

"Your recovery is strong today."

"You have been carrying some stress this week."

"A good window for something harder."

"Only you can see this."

We never say

"Great job, you crushed it!"

"Warning: elevated stress detected."

"Time to smash your goals!"

"Your data is 100% secure, guaranteed."
