Rationale

Why this line, set this way

The line does the argument in four words. "Everything sensed" is an admission of total capture. "Nothing shared" is an absolute privacy promise. Placed together they are meant to reassure, and the reassurance only works if you accept the first half. The campaign never has to accuse the reader of anything, because the sentence has already asked them to trade one for the other.

No imagery, by decision rather than style. Removing photography removes the model who would otherwise absorb the attention, so the reader is left alone with the product's voice. In a category that fills its advertising with bodies mid-stride, an empty page reads as confidence.

  • Weight. Inter at 200 rather than the 300 used elsewhere in the system. At poster scale the lighter weight keeps the tone calm and stops the line from shouting.
  • Tracking. Tightened to -0.035em so the words hold together as statements rather than drifting apart.
  • The full stops. Set in Olympian blue, the colour reserved throughout the brand for live signal. The brand guideline states that the full stop is part of the voice, calm and final and certain, so the campaign makes that literal.
  • Line breaks. Stacking isolates "sensed" and "shared" on their own lines, which are the two words the whole argument turns on.

Two formats, one sentence. The poster stacks the line so it is read slowly, and the billboard sets it on a single line to be read at a glance from distance. Neither adds an image or a second idea, because the sentence is already doing both halves of the argument on its own.
