03 · The Booklet

"Your Wellbeing, Understood".

A small booklet written as a warm, reassuring welcome to a life of being continuously sensed.

Your Wellbeing,
Understood.

A short guide to being known.

Booklet · cover · 105 × 148 mm

Page 1 · Always with you

TRACE never asks you to check in, log anything, or remember it exists. It simply stays with you, sensing gently and continuously, so that the picture of you is never a snapshot. It is a story, told in full.

Page 2 · Nothing to perform

You do not need to be your best self for TRACE. Anxious mornings, restless nights, the days you barely move. It sees all of it without judgement, because understanding you was never about improving you.

Page 3 · Yours alone

Everything TRACE learns is encrypted and belongs to you. No employer, no insurer, no one else will ever read it. Being understood this completely is a gift you are giving yourself, and only yourself.
