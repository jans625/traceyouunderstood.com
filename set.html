Rationale

Selling the objection back to the reader

This set drops the defence. The first campaign offered a trade, everything sensed for nothing shared. The second admitted that even your pauses are read. This one states that the device never stops watching and then, in three words, converts that into the reason to buy. "That's the point" is the most honest line in the whole project and also the most manipulative, which is precisely why it belongs here.

  • Every line is a complete sentence. No clauses, no qualifiers, nothing to argue with. The full stops do the persuading, which is why they are the only element carrying the accent colour.
  • Three different registers. One boasts, one comforts, one claims foresight. Together they cover the ways this category talks: confidence, relief, and quiet authority.
  • The tonal flip. Poster 02 is the only near white execution in the set. Putting the most intimate promise on the lightest ground is deliberate, since warmth is what makes it slip past.
  • Nothing is exaggerated. Every claim here is one the product, as designed, could actually make. That is the test the project has to keep passing.
